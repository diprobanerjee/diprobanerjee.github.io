Research

System identification

What can an imperfect simulator teach a real robot?

My experimental platform is a planar waterbomb-origami arm with 14 sections and two tendon actuators. Pulling a tendon changes the shape of the whole arm. I want to predict that shape from the actuator inputs using as little hardware data as possible.

I use simplified simulations of arms with different geometries to learn a shared representation of their motion. In these learned coordinates, I study whether a linear model can be identified for a physical arm from a single recorded trajectory. The test is how accurately it predicts shapes on trajectories it has never seen.

Bio-inspired motion

How does a bend travel down an octopus arm?

I explore neural-field models in which local excitation and slower adaptation produce a pulse that travels from base to tip. The question is whether this simple mechanism can explain how activation progresses along an arm during reaching.
